Choosing an Analytics Approach

Are you REALLY taking this to production,
with real data volumes?

Aggregation Pipeline

$unionWith at query time — nothing to build or operate

Is a scheduled refresh (hourly or nightly) fresh
enough, without competing with the operational workload?

Materialized Views

$merge on a schedule — computed once, served many times

Do you need to integrate this with data from
outside MongoDB, across years of history?

ASP with $iceberg

Iceberg tables on S3 — read by Athena, Snowflake or Databricks

Do you need VERY LOW latency, with no
transformation of the data?

Atlas Triggers

Atomic $inc per document, per event — no window, no $group

Do the metrics go beyond sums and counts —
averages, distinct counts, top-N?

Stream Processing

Windowed $group, continuous, seconds of lag
Still Stream Processing — needing a transformation already ruled Triggers out.
Most production answers are two of these — Stream Processing for today's numbers, Materialized Views as the nightly reconciliation of record.
